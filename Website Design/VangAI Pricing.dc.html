/ FAQ

Pricing questions

Why do you show a "from" price? {{ faqSign0 }}
Because the right setup depends on your business: how many channels, how many calls and messages, which calendar you use. After a short call you get one fixed monthly price.
Are there hidden costs? {{ faqSign1 }}
No. You pay the setup (on quote) and the fixed monthly price. No cost per call, per message or per booking.
Can I cancel anytime? {{ faqSign2 }}
We work with a minimum term of 3 months. Your customers need a little time to get used to a new way of reaching you, and after three months the numbers show clearly what the assistant brings in. After that you can cancel monthly.
Can I start with one channel and add more later? {{ faqSign3 }}
Yes. Many businesses start with one channel and add the others once they see the results. Switching to the bundle is always possible.
What is included in the setup? {{ faqSign4 }}
Everything needed to go live: learning your business, writing the conversations, connecting your channels and calendar, testing with you and launching.
What does "an update" mean? {{ faqSign5 }}
Any change to what your assistant knows or says: new prices, holiday hours, a new service, a different answer to a question. Send it to us and we make the change.
Can I try it first? {{ faqSign6 }}
Yes. On a short call we show you a demo built around your own business, free and without obligation.
Do you send appointment reminders? {{ faqSign7 }}
Yes. Your customers get a reminder before their appointment, so fewer people forget. It is an add-on for €25 a month, and included for free in the bundle.

Wondering what it would cost for you?

A short call, no sales pitch. You get a fixed monthly price, and we will tell you honestly if it is not a fit.
