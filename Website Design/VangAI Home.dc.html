/ FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Is this a chatbot with a script? {{ faqSign0 }}
No. It holds a real conversation, understands follow-up questions and answers the way your business would. No "press 1 for opening hours".
Does it tell people it is an AI? {{ faqSign1 }}
Yes, in the first sentence. The law requires it, and we think it is simply honest. Then it gets on with helping.
What if it does not know the answer? {{ faqSign2 }}
It says so and passes the question to you with the customer’s name and number, so you can call back. It never makes things up.
How long before it is live? {{ faqSign3 }}
VangChat is usually live within a few working days. VangMessage and VangVoice take one to two weeks, because WhatsApp and phone forwarding need to be approved and set up.
Does it work in French and English too? {{ faqSign4 }}
Yes. Dutch, French and English are standard, and it switches to the language the customer speaks.
Do I keep my current phone number? {{ faqSign5 }}
Yes. Calls you do not pick up are forwarded to your assistant. Nothing changes for your customers.
Do I need to be good with technology? {{ faqSign6 }}
No. We set everything up and keep it running. If you can send us a WhatsApp, you can work with us.
Can I change what it says later? {{ faqSign7 }}
Yes. Send us your changes, like new prices, holiday hours or a new service, and we update it for you every week.
What happens to the conversations? {{ faqSign8 }}
Everything is handled under GDPR. Your customer data belongs to you and is never used to train other systems. Full explanation on Data & privacy.
What does it cost? {{ faqSign9 }}
From €50 a month for one channel, excl. VAT. The exact price depends on your business, and we give you a fixed price on a short call. See pricing.
