/ About us

We build the front desk small businesses never had time for.

Small businesses lose customers every day, not because they do bad work, but because nobody was free to answer. We fix that, without asking the owner to become a tech company.

/ Our mission

We build and personally set up one assistant that answers calls, WhatsApp messages and website chats for Belgian businesses, in the languages their customers actually speak, so the owner never has to configure or manage it.

/ Our vision

A Belgium where no small business, whatever its size or technical skill, loses a customer because nobody was available to answer.
